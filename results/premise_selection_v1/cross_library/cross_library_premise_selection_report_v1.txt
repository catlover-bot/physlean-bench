Interpretation
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The raw structural graph feature alone does not outperform BM25 in either library.
2. Physlib shows a robust Top-20 gain from the import-aware dual-channel ranking.
3. CSLib does not reproduce the Top-20 gain, but does show statistically supported Top-50 and imported-Top-50 gains.
4. CSLib Top-10 recall decreases, showing that dependency transfer can improve broad coverage while disturbing early precision.
5. The cross-library evidence supports a conditional rather than universal claim: dependency transfer is useful, but its optimal reranking depth and strength depend on library structure.
6. Structure-aware Safe v2 is post-hoc exploratory analysis and must not be reported as an untouched-test result.

Important: Recall@K measures gold-premise retrieval, not end-to-end Lean proof success.
